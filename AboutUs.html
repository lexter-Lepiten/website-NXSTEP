About us

NX-STEP is a professional LMS administration and back-office support provider dedicated to helping Japanese businesses manage and optimize their e-learning systems.

We started with a simple goal – to make learning management easier, smarter, and more aligned with the way people work in real companies.

Mission

Our mission is to empower Japanese companies by managing their e-learning systems with precision, consistency, and care.

We believe that when learning is well supported, organizations grow stronger – not only in knowledge but also in culture and efficiency.

Vision

  • Professionalism: We uphold high standards in every task.
  • Clarity: We work to make communication is simple, accurate, and culturally appropriate.
  • Commitment: We deliver consistent results and continuous improvement.
  • Respect: We value every client relationship as a partnership built on trust.

Core Values

Integrity – We operate with honesty and transparency.

Excellence – We strive for the highest standards in everything we do.

Collaboration – We succeed together, as partners and as a team.

Innovation – We embrace technology to enhance our clients' success.
